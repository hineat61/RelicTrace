The Investigation

History Is Waiting to Be Discovered

RelicTrace transforms real historical artifacts into an interactive challenge of observation, geography, and historical reasoning.

I

Examine

Study an authentic artifact from the Smithsonian's collections. Look for clues in its appearance, materials, and craftsmanship.

II

Investigate

Consider the historical period and geographic region in which the artifact may have originated.

III

Discover

Submit your answer, reveal the artifact's documented history, and see how accurately you traced its origins.

Privacy Policy

RelicTrace is a historical artifact investigation game developed by Lordenshire Studios.

We respect your privacy and are committed to explaining how information is handled when you use our application.

Effective Date: October 12, 2026

1. Information We Collect

RelicTrace does not require users to create a separate account or provide personal information directly to Lordenshire Studios.

The application may store gameplay progress, scores, preferences, and related information locally on your device.

2. Apple Game Center

RelicTrace integrates with Apple Game Center to provide leaderboards and related gameplay features.

When Game Center is enabled, gameplay scores and participation statistics may be submitted to Apple's Game Center service.

Game Center authentication and associated account information are managed by Apple.

Your use of Game Center is also subject to Apple's applicable privacy policies.

3. Smithsonian Open Access

RelicTrace uses historical artifact information and imagery made available through Smithsonian Open Access.

The application may communicate with Smithsonian services to retrieve artifact records, descriptions, and images.

Such requests may involve standard technical information transmitted as part of internet communications.

4. Local Data Storage

RelicTrace may save game history, completed investigations, scores, settings, and other gameplay information on your device.

This information supports gameplay continuity and application functionality.

5. Third-Party Services

RelicTrace uses services provided by Apple and the Smithsonian Institution.

These services may process technical information according to their own privacy practices.

6. Children's Privacy

RelicTrace does not require children to submit personal information directly to Lordenshire Studios.

Game Center functionality is managed through Apple's account and parental control systems.

7. Data Security

We aim to minimize the personal information required to operate RelicTrace.

Communications with supported online services use the security mechanisms provided by those services and the operating system.

8. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated to reflect changes in application functionality, third-party services, or applicable requirements.

Updates will be published on this page.

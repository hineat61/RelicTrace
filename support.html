Getting Started

What is RelicTrace?

RelicTrace is a historical investigation game in which players examine authentic museum artifacts and attempt to determine when and where those objects originated.

Artifacts are drawn from the Smithsonian Open Access collection.

How do I play?

Examine the artifact presented on screen. Look for clues in its appearance, construction, materials, and design.

Depending on the selected investigation mode, you will estimate:

  • WHEN: The historical date or period of origin.
  • WHERE: The geographic origin of the artifact.
  • WHEN + WHERE: Both its historical date and geographic origin.

Your score reflects the accuracy of your investigation.

What is RelicTrace Classic?

Classic offers investigations drawn from the available artifact collection.

A standard Classic game contains five rounds.

What is RelicTrace: Curated?

Curated offers themed investigations organized around historical eras or geographic regions.

Curated games generally contain ten rounds, although shorter sessions may occur when fewer eligible artifacts are available.

Research & Historical Accuracy

Where do the artifacts come from?

RelicTrace uses artifact records and imagery from Smithsonian Open Access.

Historical dates and geographic origins are based on available museum catalog information.

Why are some artifact dates approximate?

Historical objects are not always associated with an exact year of creation.

Museums may identify an artifact's origin using a century, historical period, or estimated date range.

RelicTrace accounts for these uncertainties when constructing investigations.

Why are some artifacts unavailable?

Not every museum record contains enough information for a fair historical investigation.

RelicTrace selects artifacts based on the information available for the chosen investigation mode.

Game Center & Leaderboards

How do I appear on the leaderboards?

RelicTrace supports Apple Game Center leaderboards for eligible completed games.

Make sure you are signed in to Game Center on your Apple device.

Leaderboard availability may depend on your Game Center account and network connection.

My score isn't appearing. What should I do?

  • Confirm that you are signed in to Apple Game Center.
  • Check your internet connection.
  • Make sure your game was completed.
  • Close and reopen RelicTrace.
  • Allow time for Game Center to update leaderboard rankings.

Some leaderboard submissions may be retried when connectivity becomes available.

Troubleshooting

Artifacts or images aren't loading.

Some artifact information and images may require an active internet connection.

If an artifact does not load:

  • Check your network connection.
  • Try starting a new investigation.
  • Close and reopen the application.
  • Try again later if museum services are temporarily unavailable.

The application isn't responding.

Try closing RelicTrace completely and reopening it.

If the issue continues, restart your device and check for available application updates.

Do I need a separate account?

No. RelicTrace does not require a separate application account.

Apple Game Center is used for supported leaderboard features.
